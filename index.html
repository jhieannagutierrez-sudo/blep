a little letter for you

sixteen.

for the boy whose existence became one of the little things i’m endlessly grateful for.

16TH BIRTHDAY · ♡

happiest 16th, bebe ko!

now that you’re entering another year of your life, i hope today reminds you of how loved and appreciated you are—not just by me, but by everyone lucky enough to have you in their lives.

i hope you remember that you don’t have to have everything figured out yet. you’re still growing, still learning, still becoming. there will be days when you feel like you’re doing so well, and there will be days when you question yourself and wonder if you’re enough. when those days come, i hope you remember how much you’ve already grown and how many people are quietly rooting for you. so please be gentle with yourself, okay? you deserve the same kindness that you so naturally give to the people you care about.

and if there’s one thing i want you to know today, it’s that i’m so, so proud of you. not only for the things you accomplish, but for the person you are when nobody is watching. for the little ways you care, the things you do without expecting anything in return, the way you try even when you’re tired, and even the parts of you that you sometimes think aren’t worth loving. i hope you learn to see yourself through the eyes of the people who love you, because maybe then you’d understand just how precious you are.

bebe, sana sa pagpasok mo sa panibagong taon ng buhay mo, hindi mo makalimutang pangarapin yung mga bagay na nagpapasaya sa’yo. ‘wag mong madaliin yung sarili mo. may sarili kang oras, sariling daan, at sariling kuwento na sinusulat. hindi mo kailangang maging katulad ng iba para maging sapat.

and if life ever becomes too loud, i hope you remember that there are people who would gladly sit beside you in the quiet.

i hope you know that you never have to pretend to be okay just because you think you have to be strong all the time. you can rest. you can be unsure. you can have bad days. you can take your time.

hindi nababawasan ung halaga mo just because sa mga araw na hindi mo kayang maging pinakamahusay na version of yourself.

i’m also really thankful that somewhere along all the twists and turns of life, our paths crossed. out of all the people we could have met, somehow, i met you. and out of all the little moments that could have meant nothing, somehow, they became memories i hold so close to my heart.

hindi ko man maipangako na magiging madali ang lahat ng darating, maipapangako ko na pahahalagahan ko ang bawat sandaling ipinagkakatiwala mo sa’kin.

i want to know the versions of you that you haven’t met yet. i want to hear about the dreams you’ll have someday, the things you’ll discover, the places you’ll go, and the person you’ll eventually become.

i hope sixteen is kind to you. i hope it gives you reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, moments you’ll remember years from now, people who make you feel understood, and experiences that help you discover more of yourself. i hope you find courage when things get difficult and peace when everything feels overwhelming. and whenever you accomplish something, no matter how small, i hope you let yourself be proud.

and if dumating man ung mga araw na makalimutan mong mahalin ung sarili mo, ipapaalala ko sa’yo. kung posible, ipapahiram ko pa sa’yo ‘yung dalawang mata ko para malaman mo kung paano kita nakikita.

i’ll always be grateful for your existence, bebe. not because of what you can give me, not because of what you do for me, but simply because you are you. your existence alone is something worth celebrating today.

so please enjoy your day, pretty boy ! :P eat lots, laugh lots, let yourself be spoiled a little, and don’t forget to make a wish whoot whoot.

sixteen looks like the beginning of so many things— i hope you welcome them with an open heart.

and wherever this year takes you, i hope you never forget:

mahal kita hindi lamang sa mga araw na madali kang mahalin, kundi pati sa mga araw na ikaw mismo ay nahihirapang intindihin ang sarili mo.

i love you in the ordinary moments, in the silly ones, in the silent ones, and in the moments where words aren’t enough to explain what we feel. i love knowing you, learning you, and discovering all the little pieces that make you who you are.

happy birthday, bebe ko. i love you more than these words could ever properly hold :’)
